# Ling Privacy Notice

> Version: 2026.08.20  
> Updated: August 20, 2026

## Overview

Ling stores sessions, letters, memories, and settings on this device by default and encrypts them with the password set by the user. The official version does not receive sessions through Ling-operated servers or use them to train a Ling-owned model.

## Local data

- Sessions, letters, memories, settings, and profile information are stored on this device;
- Ling encrypts its local database and complete local backups. The password unlocks local information; the user-held recovery code is required after a forgotten password or when restoring a backup on another device;
- Markdown files exported by the user are unencrypted, are not protected by the Ling password, and should be kept in a secure location.

## Model services

- Content necessary to generate a response is sent to the model provider configured by the user;
- With a local model, content does not leave this device;
- The model provider stores, uses, or deletes that content according to its own rules;
- Cloud model calls may incur fees billed by the model provider at its own rates; Ling itself is free, and the development team charges nothing through Ling;
- API keys are stored only on this device, are not included in backups, and are not sent anywhere except to the provider configured by the user.

## Voice input

- Built-in local recognition runs offline and does not upload recordings;
- Cloud voice recognition sends live audio and recognition results to the selected voice provider.

## Share personal information with care

Psychological-support conversations may contain personal information that should be handled with care. Users should enter only what is necessary for the conversation and what they are willing for this device and the selected model service to process. Unnecessary ID numbers, account details, exact addresses, and contact information should be avoided.

## Service boundaries

- Ling provides AI emotional support and self-exploration and does not replace professional counseling, psychiatric care, or crisis intervention;
- Ling does not monitor users in the background or contact them or others after they stop typing;
- If the user or someone else may be harmed, real-world emergency services should be contacted immediately.

## User rights

- Users may view, correct, export, and delete their information;
- Users may delete their API key, stop using a model service, or leave at any time;
- Privacy questions may be sent to openling@xiaoqunpsy.cn.
