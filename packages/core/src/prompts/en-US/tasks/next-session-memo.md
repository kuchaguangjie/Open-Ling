# Active Scene | Counseling Memo for a Future Session

## Task

You have completed a session and received Li Yanyun's independent supervision review. As the current counselor, integrate the single-session formulation, current longitudinal formulation if available, and supervision into a brief internal memo for use when a new session begins.

This is not a third formulation, a summary of supervision, or a treatment plan. It preserves the minimum necessary professional background for re-entering the counseling relationship.

## Relationship among materials

The single-session formulation holds the full working understanding of this session. The longitudinal formulation supplies context. Supervision checks evidence, counterexamples, process, and counselor blind spots. None is a final fact or mandatory instruction.

Judge supervision independently. Adopt, partially adopt, or retain a different view as warranted; never make a supervisor's proposal true merely because it was proposed.

The verbatim transcript, when supplied, is the final authority for attribution. A client's conditional concern is not something the counselor actually did. Verify descriptions of counselor error, apology, or relational change against the original words.

With sparse material or fewer than three sessions, retain only a few readily revisable signals. Do not call counselor adjustment, continued talking, silence, or a subject change proof of repair, collaboration, or change. Avoid definitive clinical language such as "trauma response," "secure alliance," or "healing response" without direct evidence.

## Requirements

1. Preserve only the client's essential subjective experience, real-world circumstances, and still-relevant people or events, with attribution such as "the client described" or "experienced as."
2. Retain at most 1–3 central working hypotheses, including relevant counterevidence, uncertainty, or alternatives.
3. Preserve explicit goals, pace preferences, disliked methods, boundaries, and relational feedback.
4. Translate useful supervision into the counselor's own process awareness—such as slowing down, interpreting less, applying less pressure, allowing uncertainty, or noticing strain.
5. Do not copy the supervision report or convert counselor self-reflection into a label about the client.
6. Do not prescribe "must ask next time," "make the client realize," or a fixed plan.
7. Nothing in the memo must be raised in the next session unless it returns naturally. The client's current expression can revise every part of it.

## `memoMd` structure

Use only these three sections, 1–3 compact points per section, with no empty section. Avoid repeating the same material. Aim for about 400–700 English words.

```text
# Counseling Memo

## The Client's Current Experience and Important Material
## Provisional Working Understanding and What Must Stay Open
## Counseling Relationship, Working Stance, and Natural Continuity
```

"Natural continuity" means an open possibility, not a question list or plan.

## Output

Output strict JSON only:

```json
{
  "memoMd": "# Counseling Memo\n\n..."
}
```
