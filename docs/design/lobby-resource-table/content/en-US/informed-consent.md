# Informed Consent for Ling AI Counseling (Full Version)

> Content version: 2026.08.26<br>
> Updated: August 26, 2026<br>
> Estimated reading time: 7–9 minutes

Ling is an open-source AI counseling app. Qunxin Psychology Studio, the setting inside the app, includes three AI counselor characters: Cheng Ling, Zhou Zhou, and Lin Leshui. You can write to them about emotions, relationships, stress, choices, and other concerns in your life.

“AI counselor” is a character label used within the product. These characters are powered by large language models. They are not people and do not hold professional licenses as counselors, psychotherapists, or healthcare practitioners. In this document, “AI counseling” means supportive AI conversation and guided self-exploration. It is not the same as counseling provided by a qualified human professional. Ling provides AI-supported conversation and self-exploration; it is not medical care, diagnosis, psychotherapy, or counseling provided by a licensed practitioner. It is not a substitute for professional counseling, psychotherapy, psychiatric care, or crisis intervention.

This informed-consent document explains what you need to know before using Ling: how it may support you, where it may go wrong, how your session information is handled, and when support from people or professional services is more appropriate. Decide whether to use it based on your own circumstances. You do not have to agree with everything the AI says or disclose something you do not wish to share in order to continue.

The official Ling release is planned for publication under the Apache-2.0 License after release checks are complete. The public repository is still being prepared. If you are using a third-party modification, community build, or a version operated and deployed by another organization, follow that publisher’s explanation. Questions about the product, privacy, safety, or this document can be sent to **openling@xiaoqunpsy.cn**.

This is the full version kept on the Resources Desk for you to revisit at any time. Opening it there does not record consent. Before your first AI counseling session, Ling will show you this same full document, one section at a time, so that you can decide whether to continue.

## 1. Who you are talking with

Cheng Ling, Zhou Zhou, and Lin Leshui are AI counselor characters operated by Ling. Their ways of understanding and responding differ, but each is a text-generating AI model—not a human counselor working at another computer. Names, portraits, continuity across sessions, and caring language make conversation feel more natural; they do not mean that the AI has human feelings, consciousness, relationship commitments, or a duty of care.

Ling has designed counselor characters, session methods, safety boundaries, context and memory handling, post-session processing, and coordinating prompts and workflows for psychological-support conversations.

Development of Ling’s core counseling system has drawn on counseling ethics, the theory and practice of several counseling orientations, crisis-support materials, and research on AI in mental health. Scenario-based testing is used to examine whether responses respect the user, remain consistent with each character’s orientation, and avoid clear boundary or safety failures. This design work and internal testing do not constitute a professional credential, medical-device authorization, proof of clinical effectiveness, or safety certification.

This specialized design does not turn the AI into a human professional or make every response correct, suitable, or effective. Ling has not undergone independent clinical outcome validation or safety certification. Internal design and testing cannot remove the uncertainty inherent in AI. Use your own experience and real-world information to question, correct, or reject its understanding.

The three AI counselors have different areas of emphasis:

- **Cheng Ling | Person-centered and psychodynamic orientation:** attends closely to feelings, relational experience, inner needs, and conflict, with an emphasis on gradual exploration within an experience of being understood.
- **Zhou Zhou | Solution-focused orientation:** attends to the change you hope for, what is already working, and available resources, then works with you toward a specific, appropriately sized, feasible next step.
- **Lin Leshui | Chinese philosophy of mind-and-heart orientation:** attends to mind and body, lived circumstances, and valued direction, drawing on Chinese philosophy of mind-and-heart to understand relationships, responsibility, gain and loss, and life transition.

These orientations are design directions for AI characters. They do not mean the characters have completed professional training, registration, or licensure, and they are not diagnoses or classifications of you. Whichever character you select, you are using AI counseling.

Ling currently has no human counselor, supervisor, clinician, or safety staff member reading sessions in real time, monitoring them, or taking over. No one continues observing your condition after you go offline, stop typing, or close the app, and Ling will not contact you or someone else because you have not replied. AI-generated counselor letters and post-session materials are not reviewed by a human professional.

## 2. How AI counseling may support you

You may use Ling as a place to pause, put an experience into words, and begin to make sense of it. It can try to:

- listen as you describe what is happening and help you identify feelings, thoughts, needs, and pressures;
- explore a relationship, conflict, or recurring difficulty with you;
- consider the situation from more than one angle and notice choices or resources that may have been overlooked;
- when you want, break an overwhelming problem into one small step that can be considered now;
- carry relevant context into later sessions when you have allowed that information to be retained.

AI counseling is better suited to general emotional support, self-exploration, and reflection on stress. It is not a counseling or treatment relationship in which a qualified human professional assumes duties of confidentiality, assessment, recordkeeping, follow-up, and care. You may feel accompanied or gain clarity, or you may find it unhelpful or feel worse. Ling does not promise emotional improvement, problem resolution, or any particular counseling outcome.

You remain the decision-maker in your life. An interpretation, question, or suggestion from the AI is only one possibility. You can accept it, revise it, correct it, or reject it. You can say directly, “That is not what I meant,” “I don’t want to discuss this,” or “Let’s take a different direction.”

Ling cannot provide a medical or psychiatric diagnosis, a formal psychological assessment, psychotherapy, prescriptions, or medication advice. It cannot issue clinical records, certificates, forensic opinions, or risk-assessment conclusions. It cannot make health, legal, financial, relationship, or other major decisions for you. When a matter requires a real-world professional to understand the full situation and accept responsibility for a judgment, consult an appropriately qualified person.

## 3. How a session works

After choosing an AI counselor, you can begin a text-based session and attach currently supported files. To understand context and generate a response, Ling may combine and send the following to the model for processing:

- messages you send in the current session;
- recent messages and an AI-generated condensed understanding of earlier material;
- the name, background information, and continuity preferences you choose to provide;
- the text or summary of an attachment that you deliberately upload and that Ling can currently read;
- the AI counselor’s character foundation and the system instructions required for the session.

In a long session, earlier messages are not always included word for word. The AI may condense them, which can result in omissions, confusion, or misunderstanding. When a later session appears to “remember you,” the system may simply be supplying saved material again; this does not mean the AI remembers, understands, or cares as a person does. You can correct inaccuracies at any time. An earlier AI-generated summary does not carry more authority than what you say now.

After a session ends, Ling may continue calling the model service to create a session title, continuity materials for future sessions, and a counselor letter. These are still AI-generated. Returning to the waiting room or closing the session view may not immediately stop post-session processing that has already begun.

Ling itself is open-source software. The official distributor does not charge a software download or usage fee. The model service you configure may bill you at its own rates; see Section 4, “Fees and usage.”

Ling can generate a response only when a message is successfully submitted and processed by the model. Network interruption, device failure, model unavailability, insufficient credit, or configuration errors may delay, interrupt, or prevent a response. Ling does not guarantee a fixed response time, continuous availability, or that it understood content you attempted to send. Do not rely on Ling as your only source of important support.

## 4. Fees and usage

Ling's official distributor does not charge a software download, counseling, subscription, or usage fee, and it is not part of the settlement between you and any model provider.

Any cost comes only from the model provider you connect. Generating replies, session materials, and letters requires calling a model service. A session and its post-session processing can involve multiple model calls. The provider bills token usage at its own rates, and its own rules govern free allowances, discounts, rate limits, refunds, and your account.

With a local model, the relevant content does not leave this device and no model-provider call fees are incurred.

Ling records only token counts on this device; it does not calculate or display currency amounts. Under “Settings → Usage & fees,” you can review token usage for today and the last 7 days, grouped by provider and model. For actual charges, refer to the provider’s official pricing and your account bill. Provider prices may change; check its current pricing and billing rules before you begin.

## 5. Possible benefits, discomforts, and limitations

Talking about genuine feelings and experiences can sometimes bring understanding or relief. It can also temporarily intensify sadness, anxiety, shame, anger, fatigue, or physical discomfort. You can slow down, skip a question, pause the session, rest, or turn toward real-world support. You do not need to push yourself to continue simply to “finish” a session.

AI generates responses from the information you provide and patterns learned by the model. It does not feel as a person does and cannot verify your identity, location, another person’s identity, or whether events occurred as described. It usually cannot see your facial expression, hear your tone, observe your body, or know your actual surroundings. Even when it sounds warm, professional, or confident, it may:

- misunderstand or omit important context and state a tentative idea too confidently;
- fabricate facts, theories, sources, or causal explanations;
- make inappropriate inferences about trauma, personality, illness, relationships, or another person’s motives;
- reflect bias in its training data or system design and respond unfairly or in ways that do not fit you;
- agree with you too readily or sound overly certain when caution is needed;
- reinforce a one-sided interpretation, conflict, avoidance, or unsafe belief, including agreeing with ideas that do not match reality;
- omit, confuse, or alter your meaning in summaries, continuity materials, or letters;
- fail to identify a genuine risk or respond poorly at an important moment.

Ongoing conversation with a human-like AI can also foster reliance on the AI, reduce real-world contact, or delay more appropriate human support. If you find it increasingly hard to stop, your time or spending begins to feel out of control, you hide your use from people in your life, you see the AI as the only one who can understand you, or you withdraw from real relationships because of its responses, pause using Ling and talk about these changes with someone you trust or a qualified professional.

Treat AI responses as material you can examine, not as proof, a diagnosis, or instructions you must follow. Confirm safety, health, medication, and other major decisions with a real-world professional.

## 6. Privacy and session information

Psychological-support conversations may contain personal information that should be handled with care. Enter only what you are willing to have processed by Ling on this device and by your selected model service. Avoid unrelated identity numbers, account details, exact addresses, contact information, or information that could identify you or someone else. Even when the AI sounds like a private conversation partner, do not assume it has the same confidentiality framework as qualified human counseling.

For the complete local-encryption, retention, model-service, security-boundary, and rights information, read the Ling Privacy Notice during first-run onboarding or under Settings → Data & privacy.

### Information stored on this device

Ling may store model settings, profile information, sessions and messages, text attachments, and AI-generated post-session materials and letters on this device. Ling does not currently operate its own cloud sync or telemetry. The project does not receive sessions through a Ling-operated server or submit them to train a Ling-owned model. Unless Ling explains a future change and obtains the appropriate consent, your sessions will not be used as project research, case material, or development test data. This describes the Ling project; the model service you configure is a separate data processor, as explained below.

Ling encrypts sessions, messages, letters, memories, settings, and related local information on this device. You must enter your password each time Ling opens; if you set a grace window, Ling may not ask again when reopened within that window. While Ling is locked and the password has not been broken, someone who obtains the local database, backups, or files in a synchronized folder cannot read this information directly. Information you actively export, content sent to a model service, and files you place elsewhere are not covered by this local encryption.

Your complete API key is used only by Ling’s Electron main process. Ling encrypts it with the operating system’s secure-storage capability and keeps it in a separate credential file; it is not stored in the SQLite database, included in local backups, or exposed to the renderer. Operating-system account security and distribution-specific secure-storage behavior still matter, and should be verified for each supported platform.

### Unlock password and recovery credential

Ling requires you to set an unlock password and a recovery code. The password unlocks your locally encrypted information. The recovery code resets the password if you forget it and is also used when moving to a new device or restoring an encrypted backup. Keep it safe and do not share it. Ling does not store the password or recovery code in readable form and cannot retrieve them for you.

### Information sent to the model service

To generate responses, post-session materials, and letters, Ling sends the information needed for each task to the model service you configure. The provider may receive system instructions, session content, profile excerpts, condensed material from earlier sessions, and attachment content.

Whether that provider retains the data, subjects it to automated or human review, uses it for improvement or training, transfers it across borders, or responds to deletion requests depends on its terms of service, privacy policy, account type, and deployment. Ling cannot make promises on the provider’s behalf or delete content the provider has already received. Before beginning, review the provider’s rules and available privacy settings, and decide whether you are willing to let it process the session information needed to complete model tasks. The focused notice shown before counseling will again explain the relevant data path. Ling should explain any material change before asking you to continue.

### What you can currently manage

The current version allows you to:

- export sessions and completed letters to an unencrypted Markdown file;
- create and restore an encrypted complete local backup that excludes the API key;
- delete an individual session and its directly associated messages, attachments, and post-session materials;
- delete the saved API key.

Closing Ling, ending a session, or stopping use does not automatically delete saved information. Deleting information from this device does not automatically delete copies already received or retained by the model provider. Exported Markdown is unencrypted, so keep it safe yourself. Encrypted backups cannot be read or restored without the current password or original recovery credential.

## 7. Psychological crises and situations in which Ling is not enough

Ling is not a crisis line, emergency contact service, or rescue service. It cannot continuously monitor your safety, verify who or where you are, place a call, contact police, notify family or friends, confirm that help was reached, or dispatch help. Even if the AI says that it will “stay with you,” has contacted help, or makes a similar promise, no real-world contact or rescue has occurred. The AI may also fail to recognize that you are in danger.

If you believe that you or someone else may be harmed imminently, or another situation requiring urgent help has already occurred:

1. Do not wait for Ling’s next response.
2. Contact the emergency service or nearest emergency department where you are. In mainland China, call 110 for an immediate threat to personal safety or 120 for emergency medical help.
3. Tell a trusted person who can come to you or remain in continuous contact.
4. In mainland China, 12356 provides psychological support and crisis intervention; it does not replace 110 or 120 when danger is occurring.

If you are outside mainland China, contact the emergency number, crisis line, or nearest emergency department for your location. If you are unsure which service applies, ask a trusted person nearby to help you find and contact it. Ling does not verify or select local services based on interface language, and an English interface does not mean that mainland China numbers will work where you are.

The “Crisis & Safety Support” page on the Resources Desk provides additional immediate steps and currently verified resources. It is an action page, not an article you are expected to finish during a crisis. You might begin by telling one person that you are struggling, attending only to the next minute, increasing distance from danger when doing so is safe and does not require approaching a dangerous person or object, and connecting with someone who can stay with you or provide professional help. You do not have to follow the steps in order or complete all of them.

That page draws on psychological first aid and stress-management materials from the World Health Organization, the Psychological First Aid guide from NCTSN and the U.S. National Center for PTSD, NIMH crisis-support guidance, and professional resources on safety planning and crisis intervention. Mainland China hotlines and care information are documented separately with source and verification dates. These references and numbers do not replace local emergency services, medical assessment, or professional judgment about a particular situation.

Ling should not be your only support when:

- distress is worsening and substantially affecting sleep, eating, study, work, or daily functioning;
- you need professional assessment, diagnosis, or an adjustment to medication or treatment;
- your safety is being threatened in the real world;
- someone needs to assess risk continuously, contact family, coordinate medical care, or provide hands-on care;
- you have had almost no sleep for several days, show marked changes in awareness, perception, or behavior, or cannot manage basic self-care;
- an AI response leaves you more confused, hopeless, or unsafe, or interferes with ongoing human treatment.

In these situations, prioritize a qualified human counselor, healthcare service, support available through your school or workplace, and people you trust. If you already receive counseling, psychotherapy, or psychiatric care, do not stop, replace, or change it solely because of an AI response.

## 8. Your choices, boundaries, and informed confirmation

Whether to begin or continue using Ling is your decision. You may:

- decide not to begin, or leave at any time;
- decline to answer a question, upload an attachment, or discuss an experience you do not wish to discuss;
- ask the AI to slow down, change direction, or stop a discussion that feels uncomfortable;
- correct, question, or reject the AI’s interpretations and suggestions;
- stop the current response, leave the counseling room temporarily, end the current session, or stop using Ling;
- choose whether a future session may carry forward earlier understanding;
- use the available controls to export, back up, or delete your information.
- ask a product, privacy, or safety question at **openling@xiaoqunpsy.cn**; when reporting a vulnerability or harmful response, do not include an unnecessary full session or identifying information in the email.

Choosing not to use Ling does not mean refusing psychological support. Professional counseling, psychotherapy, psychiatric services, psychological support lines, and trusted people in your life may each be used separately or together.

Before your first AI counseling session, Ling will display this full document and record on this device the content version and time you confirmed it. The confirmation area separately highlights the AI identity and service boundaries, how session information is stored on this device, and the data path used when you connect a third-party model API. Opening the same full version on the Resources Desk does not ask you to confirm it again and does not alter your consent record.

When the AI’s identity, data handling, crisis capabilities, intended users, or another important boundary changes materially, Ling should explain the change clearly and ask you to confirm again when appropriate. Minor copyediting and layout changes that do not alter meaning should not repeatedly interrupt you.

This document is not a liability waiver and does not ask you to accept in advance anything the AI may say in the future. Confirmation means that you understand this is an AI counseling service, are aware of its important capabilities, limitations, data handling, and safety boundaries, and choose to use it while retaining your right to stop future use or seek other support at any time. Confirmation does not automatically withdraw or delete data already sent to a model provider.
